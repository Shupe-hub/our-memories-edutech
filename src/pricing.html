Frequently Asked Questions

Do you require a deposit before starting?
Yes, we usually require a 50% deposit to secure your spot in our development queue and begin the design phase. The remaining 50% is due upon project completion and approval before we launch the website live.
How long does a typical website take to build?
Tier A (Static) sites can take 1-2 weeks. Tier B (Dynamic/CMS) sites take 2-4 weeks depending on the number of pages. Tier C (E-commerce/Web Apps) can take 4-8 weeks depending on the complexity.
Are there monthly hosting fees?
Yes. While the pricing listed here is for the design and development, you will need to pay for domain registration and hosting on an annual or monthly basis. We can handle this setup for you.
Do you offer maintenance after launch?
Absolutely. We offer ongoing maintenance packages that include software updates, security patches, daily backups, and minor content changes.
